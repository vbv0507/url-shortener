How It Works

Shorten long links in a few quick steps.

urlzap keeps the flow simple. Paste a long URL, generate a short link, share it anywhere, and check analytics whenever you need them. That is the core of a useful free URL shortener.

The same process works whether you need a url shortener for students submitting work, or a tool to shorten render links before sharing a deployment.

Step by step

1. Paste your original URL Add a project link, assignment link, form, GitHub page, or deployment URL.
2. Generate a short link Get a compact URL that is easier to send in chats, bios, resumes, and messages.
3. Track clicks Open analytics to review click counts and visit activity from the same dashboard.

Why people use it

Cleaner sharing Short links make WhatsApp, Telegram, and email messages easier to read.
Better presentation Shorter links look more polished in assignments, portfolios, and event materials.
Faster access People can copy and open compact URLs more easily than very long raw links.
Useful analytics Track what gets clicks and understand which short links are performing best.
