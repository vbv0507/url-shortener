About

Clean links for everyday sharing.

urlzap.me is a free URL shortener built to help students, developers, and fast-moving teams shorten long links without friction. The goal is simple: turn messy URLs into clean short links that are easier to share, easier to remember, and easier to track.

If you share assignment links, project demos, forms, portfolios, or deployment URLs, urlzap gives you a cleaner way to send them. It is especially useful when you need to shorten render links before sending them in chats, resumes, or presentations.

Who it helps

Students Submit assignment links and project URLs in a format that looks cleaner and feels more professional.
Developers Share long Render and Vercel links without dropping a wall of text into messages or documents.
Event organizers Share registration forms and schedules with links that are faster to type, copy, and open.

Why this free URL shortener matters

A free URL shortener is not only about making a link smaller. It improves readability, makes messages look more trustworthy, and helps people focus on the content instead of a long string of characters.

urlzap is also a practical url shortener for students. It helps shorten long links from Google Docs, GitHub repositories, deployment pages, and online forms while keeping them easier to manage.
