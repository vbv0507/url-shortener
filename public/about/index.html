About

A free URL shortener built for practical sharing.

urlzap is designed for people who share long links every day and want a cleaner, faster way to do it. Instead of dropping bulky URLs into chats, portfolios, assignments, event messages, or product demos, you can create a short link that is easier to copy, remember, scan, and track.

The product is especially useful for students, developers, small teams, event organizers, and creators who need custom aliases, QR codes, expiry controls, and analytics without extra friction.

What makes urlzap useful

Cleaner sharing Replace long raw URLs with short links that look more professional in submissions, messages, and profiles.
QR-ready links Generate QR codes so the same short URL can work on posters, slides, events, and mobile screens.
Trackable performance Review clicks and visit history without leaving the same dashboard where you created the link.

Who urlzap is built for

Students Share assignment links, portfolios, resumes, and project submissions in a cleaner format.
Developers Shorten Render, Vercel, GitHub, API docs, and demo links before sending them to teammates or recruiters.
Event organizers Share registration forms, maps, schedules, and social links with QR codes and temporary expiry settings.
Growing teams Create short links for campaigns, onboarding docs, internal resources, and fast-moving product launches.

What the product focuses on

urlzap focuses on the basics that matter most in a real shortener: clean short URLs, readable aliases, expiry controls, QR generation, and analytics that stay close to the links you create.

That makes it a strong fit when you want a free URL shortener that is simple enough for quick sharing and useful enough to keep coming back to after the first click.
