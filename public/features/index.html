Features

The link shortener features people actually use.

urlzap is designed to keep link sharing simple while still giving you the tools that matter most in everyday work: readable short URLs, custom aliases, QR codes, expiry settings, saved links, and analytics.

That combination makes it a practical free URL shortener for students, developers, teams, and event organizers who need more control than a one-click random link generator.

Feature highlights

Custom aliases Choose readable short paths for portfolio links, event forms, projects, and campaigns.
QR code generation Open and download QR codes for any saved short link when you need scan-first sharing.
Analytics and history See click counts, visit history, and your recent links from the same workspace.

What each feature helps you do

Short links Turn long URLs into compact links that look cleaner in messages, resumes, and event material.
Aliases Make links easier to remember by replacing random IDs with branded or descriptive names.
Expiry controls Set temporary links for short campaigns, limited-time forms, or event registrations.
Saved links Keep important URLs visible in your dashboard so you can reopen, copy, scan, and share them later.

Built for real workflows

These features are useful because they support how people actually share links: in chats, classrooms, product demos, poster QR codes, portfolio docs, registration forms, and deployment reviews.

Instead of juggling different tools for shortening, QR generation, and basic click tracking, urlzap keeps that flow in one place.
